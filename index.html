Why

No one needs permission to make a film anymore.

Many people with the talent are waiting for a way in.

A community with a plan can be that way in.

The approach

Focus on the main constraint

Slicing PieYour points ÷ all points = your share

No one owns it by title. Every task earns points, and points are ownership.

No one is paid up front. If the film earns money, points decide the split. Leads earn a small bonus.

The film

Psychological thriller · suspense · light horror. A feature, about 15 to 25 shoot days.

Story written by a small group, starting in January.

Inspired by Backrooms, Exit 8, Parasite, Blade Runner 2049, 1917, You Can See Everything

The invitation

No commitment today. The details come first: the contracts and what each phase needs.

Three questions to take home:

  1. Are you potentially interested in being part of this, at any level?
  2. Is there a specific role you'd want?
  3. Could you make time in 2027 for what that role needs? How and when is up to you.
